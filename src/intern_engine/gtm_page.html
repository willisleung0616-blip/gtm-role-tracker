How roles are chosen

Source. Every role is read from the employer's own job board. The link goes to the employer's posting.

Level. From the title (intern, co-op, new grad, associate, junior, SDR, BDR), or because the posting asks for two years of experience or less. "Level unclear" means neither says, so read the posting before applying. Senior, lead, director and plain manager titles are left out.

Where. From the posting's location. When that names no place, pay quoted in CAD counts as Canada and USD as the US.

Visa. Tags come only from the posting's own words, and most postings say nothing. "H-1B employer" means USCIS approved H-1B petitions for that employer; it describes the employer, not the role.
